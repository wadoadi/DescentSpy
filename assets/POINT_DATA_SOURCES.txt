Bundled point catalogue:
- UK airfield names, ICAO codes and coordinates: OurAirports, public domain.
- UK town/place names and coordinates: GeoNames, CC BY 4.0.

UK visual reporting points are loaded from the user-supplied
`UK-VRPs.csv` asset. The file includes a "Valid From" date; the app displays
that date and associated aerodrome information for each point. Confirm every
coordinate and its current validity against current official charts/AIP before
use. This app does not automatically check for AIRAC updates.

NATS publishes the UK Aeronautical Information Publication (AIP) through its
eAIP portal in AIRAC editions, with aerodrome-specific information and
effective dates. Check the current NATS UK eAIP or official chart when
validating any VRP.

The point search is offline and searches bundled/custom point names and ICAO
codes. It does not look up postcode coordinates.
